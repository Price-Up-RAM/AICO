# AI Notice

In accordance with Article 31 of Korea's Framework Act on Artificial Intelligence (AI Basic Act), we hereby notify you of the following.

## 1. This Service Is Operated by Generative AI

AICO is a service operated on the basis of generative artificial intelligence (large language models). The dialogue, voice, reactions, and action suggestions of the on-screen character are not pre-written scripts, but are generated by AI in real time.

## 2. Your Conversation Partner Is Not a Human

The character is a virtual entity and is not a real person. Even if the character expresses emotions, such expressions are generated by a program and do not constitute human emotions or judgments.

## 3. Outputs Are AI-Generated Content

The conversational responses, voice, summary/translation results, and other content displayed in this Service are outputs created by generative AI.

## 4. Limitations of AI Output

- AI responses may be factually inaccurate or based on outdated information. Always verify important matters separately.
- AI responses do not constitute professional advice in fields such as medicine, law, or finance, and do not substitute for such advice.
- The user must decide whether to execute any device operations (agent functions) suggested by the AI.

## 5. When You Are Struggling Emotionally

Conversations with the AI character cannot replace professional psychological counseling. If you are struggling emotionally or feel you are in a crisis, do not endure it alone; seek help from a professional institution.

- Suicide Prevention Hotline: 109 (24 hours, South Korea)
- Mental Health Crisis Counseling: 1577-0199 (South Korea)
- If you reside overseas, please use the emergency and counseling services available in your area.

## 6. Data Processing

For information on where your conversation data is processed (on-device processing / transmission to external AI services), please refer to the "Privacy Policy" document.
