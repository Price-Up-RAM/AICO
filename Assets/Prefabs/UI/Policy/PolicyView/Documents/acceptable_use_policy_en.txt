# AI Use Policy

This Policy sets forth the rules for the safe use of AICO's AI features (conversation generation and agent features). This Policy forms part of the Terms of Use.

## Article 1 (Prohibited Generation Attempts)

Users shall not use the AI features of this Software to generate, or attempt to generate, any of the following:

- Sexual content involving children or minors
- Non-consensual sexual synthetic media (deepfakes) depicting real persons and defamatory content
- Content that promotes violence, discrimination, or hatred against specific individuals or groups
- Code intended to compromise the systems of others, such as malware or hacking tools
- Content intended for use in deceptive practices such as fraud, impersonation, or spam
- Any other content that violates applicable laws and regulations

## Article 2 (Restrictions on Use of AI Agent Features)

The AI agent features (mouse and keyboard control, program execution, clipboard access, and screen capture) may be used only under the following conditions:

- They shall be used only on devices and accounts that the user owns or is duly authorized to use.
- They shall not be used for automated operation of, unauthorized access to, or monitoring of another person's devices, accounts, or systems.
- They shall not be used for automation that violates the terms of service of other services (e.g., automated operation on services that prohibit macros).
- Automated operation is prohibited in environments where erroneous operation could cause serious harm, such as medical or financial systems.

## Article 3 (Prohibition on Circumventing Safeguards)

Users shall not attempt to circumvent or disable the content filters, safeguards, or feature restrictions included in this Software.
